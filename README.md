## One shop. Two connected apps.

| AquaFlow Manager | AquaFlow Worker |
| --- | --- |
| Manage customers, products, delivery subscriptions and worker assignments. Review sales, payments, balances and daily performance. | Download assigned deliveries, record delivered quantities or absence, and save cash collections while working offline. |

Workers synchronize saved work with the Manager at the shop. Offline use requires prior pairing, valid access and downloaded assignments—not a continuous internet connection during delivery work.

## Features that support your daily business

- **Customer management:** contact details, delivery history and recurring water-delivery subscriptions.
- **Delivery management:** scheduled assignments, worker allocation and pending, delivered, partial or absent statuses.
- **Sales & accounts:** shop sales, credit sales, payments, customer balances and expense tracking.
- **Reports & statements:** daily dashboard, date-range reports and printable/shareable account-statement PDFs.
- **Controlled access & recovery:** worker pairing, encrypted local storage and encrypted backup with an approval-based restore process.
- **Comfortable everyday use:** mobile-friendly screens, English/Urdu support and light/dark themes.
